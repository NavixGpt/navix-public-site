# NAVIX Privacy Policy **Last updated:** 20 October 2026 ## 1. Introduction NAVIX is a digital logbook and vehicle-management application designed to help users record vehicle trips, mileage, fuel expenses, servicing and related records. This Privacy Policy explains how NAVIX handles information when you use the application. ## 2. Information We Handle Depending on the features you use, NAVIX may store: - Your name and surname. - Account authentication information managed by the sign-in provider. - Vehicle details, including registration and odometer readings. - Trip dates, distances and business or personal classifications. - Fuel transactions, expenses and service records. - Invoices, photographs and documents you choose to save. - Business or company information you choose to provide. - Backup preferences and application settings. Only provide information that is necessary for your use of the application. ## 3. Location and Trip Information If you use features that record or calculate trips, NAVIX may process trip-related information, including dates, distances and other information required by those features. Location access, where applicable, depends on the permissions you grant and the features enabled on your device. ## 4. How Information Is Used NAVIX uses information to: - Maintain your digital vehicle logbook. - Record vehicle servicing, fuel and expenses. - Generate reports and spreadsheet exports. - Support account access and application functionality. - Create and restore backups when supported features are used. - Improve reliability, protect user data and troubleshoot problems. ## 5. Storage and Backups NAVIX may store information locally on your device and may use authentication or cloud services where those features are enabled. Manual backup and restore features allow users to create and restore backup files. Users are responsible for choosing a secure storage location for exported backup files. Automatic cloud backups depend on the availability and configuration of the selected provider, the permissions granted and the implementation of the relevant integration. Selecting a cloud provider does not, by itself, mean that automatic uploads are active. ## 6. Sharing and Disclosure NAVIX does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate enabled features, such as account authentication or cloud storage. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or when necessary to protect the security of the service. Business or fleet features may involve access by authorised members of the relevant business workspace, depending on the permissions implemented for that feature. ## 7. Retention and Deletion Information may remain on your device or in backup files until you delete it. Information stored by third-party services may be subject to their retention and deletion processes. Before uninstalling NAVIX, changing devices or deleting records, create a backup if you need to preserve your information. ## 8. Security NAVIX is intended to protect user information through appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. No application, device or online storage service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices, account credentials and backup files. ## 9. Your Choices You can control the information you enter, the permissions you grant and whether you create manual backups. Availability of account-data access, correction and deletion options may depend on the features provided by NAVIX and its service providers. ## 10. Children's Privacy NAVIX is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation where required by applicable law. ## 11. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as NAVIX develops. The updated version and its effective date will be published on this page. ## 12. Contact For privacy questions or requests, contact the NAVIX team through the official contact channel published on the NAVIX website. **Please note:** Replace this contact wording with a monitored email address before public release.
